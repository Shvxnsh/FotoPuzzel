HOW TO PLAY

Pinch. Frame. Fist. Click.

Allow camera access, pinch with both hands, and move the pinch points to place the yellow frame. Spread them apart or together to resize it. When you are happy with the composition, make a fist with either hand and FotoPuzzel will capture the photo automatically.
